# Creative Notes

This MIDI pack is original generative material.

It does not include:
- copyrighted audio,
- samples,
- exact transcriptions,
- melodies from commercial releases,
- recreated arrangements from specific tracks.

It does include:
- original drum MIDI,
- original bass MIDI,
- original arrangement sketches,
- high-level stylistic traits common to hard/industrial/Birmingham techno:
  4x4 pressure, sparse bass, metallic percussion, polyrhythmic displacement,
  density changes and live-mutation arrangement logic.
